## 2. Overview (About) — English (max 2,000 characters)

AXTO builds AI software you can run yourself. Ten applications cover the work of legal, security and business
teams, and every one of them is available as a Docker app for Windows and Linux, downloaded from your AXTO
portal and activated with an official licence.

• Guardian AI — self-hosted AI cybersecurity for your servers
• Orchestra AI — one console for your AI provider keys and your CPU and GPU workers, with routing, auto and
  manual scaling, GPU temperature and load indicators, and an OpenAI-compatible API for your own apps
• Vault AI — removes personal data from prompts before they reach any model
• Edge AI — AI gateway and API management for your customers
• SOC AI — AI-assisted SIEM and SOAR
• Compliance AI — continuous, automated audit monitoring
• Sentinel OT — security for industrial IoT and OT networks
• Antivirus — self-hosted, multi-engine malware scanning
• AXTO Studio — your self-hosted AI and GPU workspace
• AXTO Legal — private AI for legal work, with 36 practice workspaces

How we work: you bring your own AI keys and pay providers directly, with no token markup. Files, documents and
personal data are processed on your own machines; AI requests are only relayed. Your GPU runs open models
locally through the GPU worker — NVIDIA, AMD, Intel and Apple cards are detected automatically.

The cloud workspace at axto.io runs in your browser, in 10 languages, with monthly and lifetime plans.

Contact: hello@axto.io · https://axto.io
