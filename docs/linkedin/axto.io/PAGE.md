## About (English, ≤2,000 characters)

axto.io is AI and cybersecurity you run yourself — a brand of PT AXTO Digital Global.

Ten applications, each a Docker app for Windows and Linux, downloaded from your AXTO portal and activated with an
official licence:

• Guardian AI — AI cybersecurity for your servers: process, network, file-integrity and DNS monitoring, incident
  management and automated response
• Orchestra AI — one console for your AI provider keys and your CPU and GPU workers: seven routing modes, auto and
  manual scaling, GPU temperature and load at a glance, and an OpenAI-compatible API for your own apps
• Vault AI — removes personal, health and financial data from prompts before they reach any model
• Edge AI — an AI gateway and API management for your customers
• SOC AI — AI-assisted SIEM and SOAR
• Compliance AI — continuous, automated audit monitoring
• Sentinel OT — security for industrial IoT and OT networks
• Antivirus — self-hosted, multi-engine malware scanning
• AXTO Studio — your self-hosted AI and GPU workspace
• AXTO Legal — private AI for legal work, with 36 practice workspaces

How it works: you bring your own AI keys and pay providers directly, with no token markup. Files and personal data
are processed on your machines; AI requests are only relayed. A GPU worker runs open models on your own graphics
card — NVIDIA, AMD, Intel and Apple cards are detected automatically.

The cloud workspace at axto.io runs in the browser, in 10 languages, with monthly plans and lifetime licences.

Contact: hello@axto.io · https://axto.io
