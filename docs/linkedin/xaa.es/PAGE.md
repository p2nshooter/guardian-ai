## About (English, ≤2,000 characters)

xaa.es is a web and software studio — a brand of PT AXTO Digital Global — working in English, Spanish and Indonesian.

What we build:
• Websites that sell — landing pages, portfolios, company profiles and corporate sites
• Commerce — from a first catalogue to multi-warehouse, multi-currency operations
• Products & SaaS — multi-tenant applications with subscriptions, billing, roles and an API
• Enterprise systems — portals, SSO, role-based access, audit trails, and AI backup & recovery your own team runs
• Election & civic systems — voter-roll verification, an offline field register and a same-evening recapitulation,
  from one village to a nation

How we work:
• You pay in steps — a 10% deposit opens the project, 40% starts production, and the last 50% is due only once you
  have seen it running
• A real completion date, published the moment your concept lands
• Live progress, a milestone ledger and a dated activity log in your client portal
• You own what we build — source code, designs, content and accounts transfer to you at handover

Packages start at €500 for a landing page; setup and backup & recovery plans start at €149. Our SaaS template
catalogue starts at €390.

Contact: https://xaa.es/contact
