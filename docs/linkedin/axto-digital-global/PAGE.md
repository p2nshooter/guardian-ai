## About (English, ≤2,000 characters)

PT AXTO Digital Global builds software people can run with confidence — on their own servers, in their own
language, under their own control.

Three brands, one standard:

axto.io — AI & cybersecurity you run yourself. Ten applications: Guardian AI, Orchestra AI, Vault AI, Edge AI,
SOC AI, Compliance AI, Sentinel OT, Antivirus, AXTO Studio and AXTO Legal. Each one is a Docker app for Windows
and Linux, downloaded from the customer portal and activated with an official licence. Customers bring their own
AI keys and pay providers directly; their files and personal data are processed on their own machines.

xaa.es — a web and software studio in English, Spanish and Indonesian. Websites, e-commerce, SaaS products,
enterprise and civic systems. Clients pay in steps, follow progress live, and own the source code at handover.

ulyah.com — an Islamic learning platform in 28 languages: the Qur'an with 32 reciters and a mushaf that follows
the recitation, translations, hadith, a digital kitab library, audiobooks, Prophets' stories, prayer times, qibla,
Hijri calendar, zakat and inheritance calculators, live recitation and lectures, Qur'an radio, a kids' corner and
Hajj & Umrah packages.

What ties them together: honest claims, software you own, and data that stays where it belongs.

Contact: hello@axto.io
